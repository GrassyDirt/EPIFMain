This Cookie Policy explains how [epif_info field="legal_name"] ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on epifservices.com (the "Site"). Please read it together with our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. They let the site remember information between pages or visits. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in a similar way.

Cookies we use

If you are simply visiting the Site, we do not set any cookies. Our newsletter and contact forms work without cookies, and the Site does not currently use analytics, advertising, or social media tracking cookies.

Cookies are set only for people who log in to manage the Site (our staff). These cookies are strictly necessary for logging in, and they are:

  • wordpress_test_cookie: checks that your browser accepts cookies when you open the login page. Expires when you close your browser.
  • wordpress_[hash] and wordpress_sec_[hash]: keep you securely logged in to the admin area. They expire when you close your browser, or after 14 days if you choose "Remember Me."
  • wordpress_logged_in_[hash]: shows that you are logged in. Same duration as above.
  • wp-settings-[user ID] and wp-settings-time-[user ID]: remember how you set up the admin screens. Expire after 1 year.
  • _lscache_vary: tells our page cache to show logged-in users fresh, uncached pages. Expires when you log out.

If this changes

If we add analytics, advertising, or other non-essential cookies, we will update this Cookie Policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before setting them. You will be able to change your choice at any time.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not affect your ability to browse the Site or use its forms, but it will prevent logging in to the admin area. Your browser's help pages explain how:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
