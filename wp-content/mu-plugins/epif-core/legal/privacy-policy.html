This Privacy Policy explains how [epif_info field="legal_name"] ("[epif_info field="brand"]," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit epifservices.com (the "Site"), sign up for our newsletter, or contact us. It also explains the choices and rights you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Newsletter signup: your email address and a record of your consent (the wording you agreed to and the date and time).
  • Contact and quote requests: your name, email address, and, if you choose to provide them, your phone number, company, the service you are interested in, and your message.
  • Other communications: information you include when you email or call us.

Please do not send us sensitive personal information (such as health, financial account, or government ID numbers) through the Site.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical and log data: when you visit the Site, our hosting provider's servers automatically record information such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, referring page, and date and time. This is used to run and secure the Site.
  • Spam prevention: when you submit a form, we store a scrambled, one-way version (a "hash") of your IP address so we can limit repeated submissions. We cannot turn the hash back into your IP address.
  • Campaign information: if you reach the Site through a link that contains marketing parameters (for example "utm_source"), we save those parameters with your form submission so we know which of our marketing is working.
  • Cookies: see our Cookie Policy for details.

How we use information

  • To send you the newsletter and updates you asked for, including our launch announcement.
  • To respond to your questions and requests, prepare quotes, and provide our services.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site, and to prevent spam, fraud, and abuse.
  • To understand which marketing brings visitors to the Site.
  • To comply with the law, and to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not sell your personal information.

Legal bases for processing (EEA and UK visitors)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on: your consent to send you our newsletter (you can withdraw it at any time); steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract when you request a quote or services; our legitimate interests in running a secure and effective website and responding to inquiries; and legal obligations where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

How we share information

We share personal information only as described here:

  • Service providers who help us run the Site and our business, under contracts that limit their use of your information to providing services to us. These include our website hosting provider; our email delivery provider, which sends emails such as newsletter confirmations; any email marketing platform we use to send the newsletter; and Akismet, a spam-filtering service operated by Automattic Inc., which may receive the name, email address, message, IP address, and browser information submitted through our contact form in order to check it for spam.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is needed to protect the rights, property, or safety of us, our customers, or others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to this Privacy Policy.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

Analytics and advertising

The Site does not currently use third-party analytics or advertising cookies. If we add tools such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel, we will update this Privacy Policy and our Cookie Policy first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before they run.

Emails and unsubscribing

We only add you to our newsletter after you confirm your email address by clicking the link in our confirmation email. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also unsubscribe by emailing [epif_info field="privacy_email"]. If you unsubscribe, we may still send you messages about a request or service you asked for.

How long we keep information

  • Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe. Signups that are never confirmed are deleted automatically after 30 days. After you unsubscribe we keep your email address and the date you unsubscribed, only so we can respect your choice.
  • Contact and quote requests: for as long as needed to respond to you and keep our business records, and then deleted.
  • Server logs: for the limited period set by our hosting provider.

Your rights and choices

Everyone can unsubscribe from our emails at any time and ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about them by emailing [epif_info field="privacy_email"]. We will need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time the law requires.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to restrict or object to our processing, to receive your information in a portable format, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting processing that already took place), and to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, or Oregon), you may have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and disclose it; to access, correct, and delete it; and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, and we do not use it for profiling that has legal or similarly significant effects. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision.

Global Privacy Control: because we do not sell or share personal information, there is nothing to opt out of today. If that changes, we will treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a valid opt-out request.

The Site is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

No website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to other websites

Changes to this Privacy Policy
