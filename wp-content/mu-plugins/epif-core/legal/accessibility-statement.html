[epif_info field="brand"] wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use epifservices.com (the "Site").

Our goal

We aim for the Site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. As we build the Site, we:

  • use clear page structure, headings, and labels that work with screen readers;
  • make sure the Site can be used with a keyboard alone;
  • choose text and background colors with enough contrast;
  • give form fields visible labels and clear error messages; and
  • test pages on phones, tablets, and computers.

Known limitations

The Site is under active development, and some pages or features may not yet be fully accessible. We are working to fix issues as we find them.

Feedback and help

If you have trouble using any part of the Site, or need information in a different format, please tell us. We will do our best to help and to get you the information you need another way.
